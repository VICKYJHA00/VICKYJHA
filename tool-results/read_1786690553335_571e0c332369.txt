    16→### Design Philosophy Overhaul
    17→| Aspect | Before (AI-feeling) | After (Human-feeling) |
    18→|--------|-------------------|---------------------|

    20→| **Typography** | Monospace everywhere, tiny text | Geist sans-serif, proper hierarchy |
    21→| **Theme** | Terminal/hacker/CLI metaphors everywhere | Clean, minimal, professional |
    22→| **Copy** | Dramatic ("Architecting the Invisible Engine") | Conversational ("I build backends that don't break") |
    23→| **Hero** | IDE mockup with code tabs + terminal | Simple centered text with name + tagline |
    24→| **About** | Glow ring portrait, "cinematic" language | Clean photo + natural bio | 
    25→| **Skills** | Pipeline metaphor + console log panel | Simple progress bars in 2x2 grid |
    26→| **Projects** | Over-decorated cards with IDE visual simulations | Clean cards with real Unsplash images |
    27→| **Timeline** | Git log terminal with commit hashes + diff view | Clean vertical timeline with colored dots |
    28→| **Contact** | Terminal-themed form with fake SMTP logs | Normal form with proper labels |
    29→| **Animations** | Heavy (cursor glow, particles, floating tech) | Subtle (fade-ins, progress bars) |
    30→| **Footer** | Complex | Minimal, 3 lines |

    51→### Key Design Decisions
    52→1. **Orange accent (#f97316)** — Distinctive, warm, NOT the typical AI blue/purple
    53→2. **No monospace in body text** — Only used for code-like contexts if any
    54→3. **Real Unsplash images** for project cards instead of AI-generated mockups
    55→4. **Conversational copy** — "Here's a rough breakdown" vs "Modular Competency Pipeline"
    56→5. **Subtle animations** — Just whileInView fade-ins, no particles/glow/cursor effects
    57→6. **Proper spacing** — py-28 for sections, max-w-6xl for content
